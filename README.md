# Make your doll
"game" made in gamemaker, where you can make your own doll, being able to choose her hair and clothes!!<br>
it's the second game i made in gamemaker, so there's a LOT of bugs yet, and i intend to add more things in the future, when i come with ideas of how to make it better! :D<br>
## Description
at this game, you can style a little doll the way you desire, with some hair styles, different combination of clothes and color them with your favorite colors!! :0 <br>
(it was inspired by the character customization of different games and gacha life >o<)<br>
